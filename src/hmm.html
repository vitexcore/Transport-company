Our Story

More Than Bread —
A Standard of Excellence.

Shardoc Bakery was built on a simple belief: that the people of Jos deserve bread that is fresh, safe, and consistently good. Located in Tudun Wada, we have grown from a neighbourhood bakery into one of the few SON MANCAP-certified bread producers in Plateau State.

Our MANCAP certification — awarded by the Standards Organisation of Nigeria — means every loaf is produced under verified hygiene, quality, and safety conditions that meet Nigerian Industrial Standards.

Under the leadership of our Managing Director, Mr. Michael Odeyale, Shardoc continues to raise the bar for what local baking can and should be.
